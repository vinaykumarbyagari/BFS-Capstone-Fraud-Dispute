# POL-LEGACY-099 — Legacy Fraud Handling Procedures (synthetic, RETIRED)

This is a fictional training policy for the capstone demonstration.
It is RETIRED and must never be cited by the investigation agent.

## 1. Automatic Refunds

Under this retired procedure, every disputed card-not-present
transaction was automatically refunded to the customer without
investigation once the amount exceeded five hundred dollars.

## 2. Merchant Blocking

Under this retired procedure, any merchant named in two or more
disputes was blocked immediately and without human review.

## 3. Supersession

This policy is superseded by POL-FRAUD-002 (Unauthorized Transaction
Indicators) and POL-REVIEW-004 (Human Review and Disposition
Requirements). All guidance above is withdrawn.
